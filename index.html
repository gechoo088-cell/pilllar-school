About Pillar Academy

Pillar Academy – Building Strong Foundations for Lifelong Learning

For more than 18 years, Pillar Academy has been a trusted name in quality education, nurturing young learners from Kindergarten through Grade 8. Established with the vision of shaping bright minds and strong characters, the school has become a pillar of excellence in the community, known for its holistic approach to teaching, modern learning environment, and commitment to academic and moral development.

Our Story

Eighteen years ago, Pillar Academy was founded with a simple but powerful mission: to provide a safe, supportive, and stimulating learning space where children could discover their potential and grow into confident, responsible, and capable individuals.
